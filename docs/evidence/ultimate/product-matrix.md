## Model integration

No model is enabled or qualified in Yamakan, and none is downloaded to this machine. One real model ran on Kaggle, with its video brought in as a file. `ENGINE_ENABLED` is false. Commercial providers are off. Nothing sends media to a cloud service today, because generation is not wired to anything; only a read-only health check reaches ComfyUI, on this machine. It refuses partner nodes and a ComfyUI not started offline; `--offline` is not an egress block. By the founder's decision a person starts it, with no Yamakan sandbox.
