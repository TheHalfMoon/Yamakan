Preserve the working SQLite, media, process, timeline and export infrastructure. Repair creative persistence and browser command cohesion before adding engines. ## Winning strategy and minimum product

1. Preserve all acknowledged creative work: Studio, scene, shot and Director state must survive reopen and verified backup/restore; security consent must remain revoked across project undo.
2. Make one editing journey excellent: import, organize, trim, ripple, retime, captions, audio, preview, export and revise. Show only parity-qualified effects and export formats.
3. Provide fast bounded proxies, thumbnails and waveforms; stop loading complete large media files into the browser for analysis. Measure before raising schema limits.
4. Put Story, Edit and Finish around one project. Camera, character and audio tools live in context; a model picker is secondary to the intended operation.
5. Let agents propose revision-bound edits and resume durable work. Human approvals bind exact outputs and cannot be synthesized by a model.
6. Later qualify one engine on an authorized capable host. Measure supported controls, complete-clip quality, failures and total cost before exposing them as available.

## What to copy, retain and decline

Adapt narrow OpenCut interaction/animation algorithms and inspected editor patterns. Wrap FFmpeg and optional media workers behind existing ownership. Use OTIO for a documented interchange subset. Study MLT/Kdenlive/Shotcut before a preview-engine migration; do not introduce their project databases or job schedulers. Keep the imported Studio engine, measure its host persistence boundary, and preserve provenance. [Donor records](08_SOURCE_DONOR_MASTER_MATRIX.md) distinguish inspected modules from deferred discovery.

Build Yamakan-native canonical documents, capability negotiation, proposal approvals, continuity/reference bookkeeping and cost reconciliation. Reject unrestricted custom nodes, whole-application amalgamation, hidden browser-only edits, invented camera adherence, automatic paid retries and a new model-training program. Defer HDR finishing, a complete compositor, simultaneous multi-engine operations and collaborative cloud editing until their narrower predecessors qualify.
